SITE FACTS — AI MECH LAB

The site is called AI Mech Lab.

The site teaches hardware only: AI, Robotics, Mechatronics, Electronics and Embedded Systems.

BUILDING PATHWAY:
The user types a project, like a drone or a robot. Then they choose:

- "Learn how to build it": a full beginner track broken into small steps.
- "Just build it": a checklist of build actions for people who already know the theory.

LEARNING PATHWAY:
The user picks a subject, like Mechatronics, and gets a track from the basics up to a confident level.

TRACK STRUCTURE:
A track is made of small steps. Each step is either:

- Video step: a video plus a written lesson.
- Manual step: a written checklist, used for things like buying parts and testing.

KNOWN SKILLS:
Skills the user already knows show as "Refresher" steps with a shorter video. The site remembers what the user has learned.

TRACK EDITING:
The user can ask for changes to their track, like removing or adding a step.

SUPPORTED TOPICS:
Goals outside hardware are not available on the site.

COST AND PLANS:
The site is free.

CERTIFICATES:
The site does not give certificates.

SUPPORT:
There is currently no support contact.

ACCOUNTS AND LOGIN:
Users need an account to use the site. They can sign up with email, LinkedIn or phone number. Their progress is saved to their account.
